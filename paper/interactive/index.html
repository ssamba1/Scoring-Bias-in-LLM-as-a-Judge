Dashboard (3 retracted)
Three of the four dashboards here have been retracted.

The Bias Explorer, Model Comparison and Model Ranking pages presented a "22-model landscape" that a 2026-07 audit found was not supported by real data: it included models that do not exist. Those pages are preserved, unaltered, under RETRACTED/paper/interactive/ for accountability rather than reuse. See DATA_INTEGRITY_AUDIT.md and RETRACTED/README.md.

The dashboard below is unaffected. It is built from the provenance-verified Kaggle T4 run over six open-weight families, which is the evidence the honest paper rests on.
